📖 Frequently Asked Questions

Wanna know something? check here!

How do I order a commission? ▾
Log in as a client, then click "Order a Treat" on the home page. You'll go through the Cake Book steps — choose your option, add details, and submit. After that, you can track your order and chat with me!!
How long does a commission take? ▾
This really depends how busy i am! just ask!
How do I pay? ▾
Currently, through cashapp! (sorry other users!) make sure to tell me the name of your cashapp! so i can confirm then unlock!
Can I make changes to my order? ▾
Minor revisions are included! Just message me in chat and we'll figure it out together!
What if the shop is "Fully Booked"? ▾
When my active order slots are full, the shop automatically shows "Fully Booked — Waitlist Open." You can join the waitlist with your email, and I'll reach out as soon as a slot opens up for you!
How do I leave a review? ▾
After your order is marked "Done" and you download the final piece, a pop-up will appear asking if you liked how it turned out. Just click the stars to rate your experience and leave a comment — I'd love to hear your thoughts! 🍒
